FIND THE 1 ORB

A Game of luck and Will

How to Play

Easy: 10 boxes, 1 orb revealed for 1 second. Click the correct box to avoid losing a life.

Hard: 20 boxes, 8 orbs revealed for 0.5 seconds. Click the correct orb to avoid losing a life.

Extreme: 50 boxes, numbers 1–10 hidden. Click all numbered orbs to complete the sequence. Wrong clicks reduce life. First player to 0 lives loses.
